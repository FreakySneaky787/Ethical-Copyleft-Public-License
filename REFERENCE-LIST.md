# ECPL Reference List (v1.3)

Institutions whose published judgments, decisions, reports, or findings count as a "finding" under
Section 3(b) of the Ethical Copyleft Public License (ECPL v1.3). This list belongs to release `v1.3` and
changes only with a new ECPL version.

## Human rights

- United Nations Human Rights Council, including its commissions of inquiry and fact-finding missions
- Office of the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights (OHCHR)
- United Nations human rights treaty bodies
- International Court of Justice (ICJ)
- International Criminal Court (ICC)
- European Court of Human Rights
- Inter-American Court of Human Rights
- African Court on Human and Peoples' Rights

## Labor exploitation and forced labor

- International Labour Organization (ILO), through its supervisory bodies

## Severe environmental destruction

- International Court of Justice (ICJ)
- International Tribunal for the Law of the Sea (ITLOS)
